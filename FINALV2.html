Common Questions

Can I use notes and a syllabus together?

Yes. Upload notes, a syllabus or both in the same upload box. Signed-in users can select up to 8 PDFs or images, with a combined size of 50 MB. On the hosted app, guest uploads can total up to 3 MB.

Can I download a question paper and answer key separately?

Yes. Each export opens a print window. Choose Save as PDF to save that document.

Where are my papers and attempts saved?

Signed-in test results and papers you choose to save sync to your account. Pending saves retry when you reconnect. Guests can generate and download, but their papers and test results are not saved.

How do I skip a question?

Press Next without selecting an answer. Use the numbered question buttons to return at any time before submission.

Can I reduce the animation?

Use Settings to turn transitions off. Your system’s reduced-motion preference is also respected.
